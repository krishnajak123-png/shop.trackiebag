How GPS tracking works

Three steps to staying connected.

01

Your child carries the bag

The GPS-enabled bag connects to the tracking system whenever it's within network and satellite range.

02

Parent opens the app

Parents can check the bag's approximate location, battery level and last-updated time through the connected app.

03

Stay connected

Receive relevant alerts, such as safe-zone entry or exit, low battery and SOS notifications, in real time.

GPS accuracy and connectivity depend on network and satellite availability, and may vary indoors, underground or in areas with weak signal.

Safety features

Every TrackieBag pairs everyday comfort with a focused set of safety features — nothing gimmicky, nothing overstated.

Live Location

Check your child's approximate location from the parent app, whenever the bag has network and GPS signal.

SOS Emergency Alert

A dedicated SOS button sends an immediate alert with the bag's last known location to registered parent contacts.

Safe-Zone Alerts

Set up zones like home or school and get notified when the bag enters or leaves them.

Battery Alerts

Location History
